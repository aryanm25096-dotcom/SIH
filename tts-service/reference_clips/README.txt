IndicF5 Reference Audio Clips Directory
===========================================

IndicF5 requires a short (5-10 second), clear, calm reference audio clip (.wav)
plus its exact transcript for each language to clone prosody and speaker characteristics.

Expected files corresponding to reference_voices.json:
- hi_ref.wav (Hindi)
- mr_ref.wav (Marathi)
- bn_ref.wav (Bengali)
- ta_ref.wav (Tamil)
- te_ref.wav (Telugu)
- gu_ref.wav (Gujarati)
- kn_ref.wav (Kannada)
- ml_ref.wav (Malayalam)
- pa_ref.wav (Punjabi)
- or_ref.wav (Odia)
- as_ref.wav (Assamese)

Audio Specs:
- Format: WAV (mono, 24kHz or 16kHz recommended)
- Duration: 5 - 10 seconds
- Voice Tone: Calm, neutral, professional healthcare assistant tone.
